The Founder

Built different
from day one.

Elwood Blues Construction was founded in 2022 by Harris Lyons, with a name that carries personal meaning. “Elwood” was the middle name of Harris’s late father, whose love for The Blues Brothers helped inspire the company’s name. It serves as a tribute to his father and a reminder to approach the business with pride, character, and purpose.

Harris brings more than a decade of residential construction experience to the company. He began his career as a finish carpenter in new home construction before transitioning into construction management, where he spent five years overseeing the production of several residential communities ranging from single-family homes to multifamily developments.

Today, that combination of hands-on construction knowledge and management experience guides Elwood Blues Construction. The company is built around quality workmanship, clear communication, transparency, and cost-conscious solutions. The goal is simple: provide clients with a straightforward construction experience and work the company is proud to stand behind.

How We Operate

Principles that
drive every project.

Accountability

Harris's name is on every contract. When something needs to be fixed, there's no question who picks up the phone. Accountability isn't a value statement — it's a structural reality.

Precision

We don't cut corners because corners are what people see. Every measurement, every material, every finish is held to the same standard — the one we'd apply to our own home.

Transparency

Fixed-price contracts. No surprise change orders. No hidden markups. You know the cost before we break ground, and we stand behind it when the project is done.
