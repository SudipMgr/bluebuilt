How We Work

A rigorous, transparent process that puts you in control from day one. No surprises. No excuses. Just steady, professional execution from first consultation to final handover.

Step by Step

Four phases.
Zero guesswork.

01
Phase One

Discovery & Consultation

Harris meets with you on-site — not a sales rep, not a project coordinator. He'll walk your space, listen to your vision, and give you an honest read on what's achievable within your budget and timeline. This is where we decide if we're a fit.

On-site visit Scope discussion Budget alignment Timeline estimate No hard sell

"Most contractors tell you what you want to hear at this stage. We tell you what we see. That honesty upfront is what protects your investment downstream."

02
Phase Two

Design & Planning

We produce detailed plans, finalize material selections, and build a clear project roadmap. Every permit, every subcontractor, every material delivery is coordinated before we break ground. You'll know the price and schedule before signing anything.

Blueprint review Material sourcing Permit coordination Subcontractor scheduling Fixed-price contract

You receive a fixed-price contract — not an estimate. The number you sign is the number you pay. No budget-busting change orders down the line.

03
Phase Three

The Build

Expert execution with full communication. Daily progress updates, site access whenever you want it, and direct contact with Harris throughout the build. Our crews treat your home like their own — maintaining cleanliness, protecting existing finishes, and solving problems before they become delays.

Daily updates Site cleanliness Quality checkpoints Milestone reviews Direct Harris access

You'll never be left wondering what happened today. If there's a decision to be made, you're in the loop before it's made.

04
Phase Four

Completion & Walkthrough

A meticulous final inspection — Harris walks every room with you. We produce a punch list together and complete every item before calling the project done. Our relationship doesn't end at handover; we stand behind our work long after you've moved in.

Final inspection Punch list completion Warranty documentation Post-project support

The project isn't complete until you say it is. We don't issue a final invoice until you've signed off on every item on the punch list.

Our Commitments

What you can
always count on.

Fixed-Price Contracts

The price we quote is the price you pay. We don't use low-ball estimates to win bids and then stack on change orders. If we encounter something unexpected, we talk to you before it costs you.

On-Time or We Fix It

We build realistic schedules and hold to them. If a delay is on us, we absorb the cost of keeping your project moving. Your time is not less valuable than ours.

Workmanship Warranty

We stand behind our work after the invoice is paid. Defects in workmanship are our responsibility to correct — no arguments, no runaround, no charge.

Common Questions

Before you reach out,
some answers.

How long does the consultation process take?

Most initial site visits take 60–90 minutes. From there, we typically turn around a detailed proposal within 3–5 business days depending on the project scope.

Do you handle permits and HOA approvals?

Yes — fully. We manage all city and county permits, and can coordinate with your HOA on your behalf. You shouldn't have to chase paperwork.

What areas of Phoenix do you serve?

We're based in Phoenix 85022 and serve the broader Phoenix metro. Call us if you're unsure — we'll let you know if your address falls within our range.

Can I make changes during the build?

Minor changes are often absorbed. Significant scope changes will be quoted transparently before we proceed — never added quietly to a final bill.

What payment options do you offer?

We accept 9+ payment methods including major credit cards, ACH transfer, financing through partner lenders, and staged payment schedules tied to project milestones.

What is your minimum project size?

We generally work on projects starting at $25,000. Below that threshold, the overhead of our planning process doesn't serve you or us well — but reach out and we'll be honest with you.
